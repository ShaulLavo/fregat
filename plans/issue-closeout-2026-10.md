## Fixed and obsolete decisions

### Issue 942

Current run.test.ts has an explicit 20-second overall SIGINT budget around its 10-second readiness poll and preserves exit 130 plus the recorded outcome. A controlled 6-second startup fails the old budget and passes the new contract. The historical delayed phase remains unknown.

### Issue 840

The transferred #936 repair merged. Current autofix.test.ts budgets the four cold processes at 15 seconds; measured loaded worst case was 12.7 seconds, with unchanged fixtures and assertions and 10/10 loaded controls after the change. This fixes the insufficient test budget without claiming the original delayed child was identified.

### Issue 554

Current cpuSample rejects nonfinite or negative stable-PID deltas with process/before/after facts; the zero clamp is gone. Raw-boundary retention is separate #654 work, whose prepared-repair claim remains untouched.

### Issue 949

Already closed by its owning lane before this closeout. The catalog dependency release-status repair merged in PR #951. No close or ownership mutation by this lane.

### Issue 895

Current Sol options use the provider catalog defaultReasoningEffort; only gpt-6-astra has the explicit Medium override. The fixture/read-only investigation found Low in the installed Codex catalog. Public API defaults describe a different request path. Fregat follows the Codex descriptor, and explicit effort choice still overrides it. No live account check was performed; a preference for Medium is a product decision, not evidence of a broken default.

## Delivery and limits

All documentation changes belong to one PR. Eligible issues are closed only after that PR opens,
with its link and the exact plan section or shipped repair. Claimed issues remain open and this
lane does not remove their labels. The original closed threads remain the permanent source of
full logs and historical artifact references.

This review changes documentation only. It does not run new benchmark, provider, Mac, browser
crash or product-reproduction loops. Existing controlled reproductions are attributed to their
issue comments and merged repairs. Dependency installation, documentation checks and normal
commit hooks are this lane's verification. Closing unconfirmed records preserves their uncertainty
and schedules the missing cause capture.
