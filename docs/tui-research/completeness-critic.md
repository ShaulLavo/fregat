### G8 `accessibility-and-plain-mode` — What is the accessible/plain mode, and is it in scope?

No report mentions screen readers, reduced motion, or monochrome. The web app has `role="status"`/`aria-live` on loaders and the chord indicator (`packages/ui/src/components/spinner.tsx`, `apps/web/src/keymap/components/pending-chord-indicator.tsx`) and CLAUDE.md requires loaders to slow rather than freeze under reduced motion; OpenTUI's type surface has no accessibility API. Alt-screen TUIs are opaque to VoiceOver/NVDA/Orca, which is one reason codex writes history into native scrollback with a copy-friendly `/raw` mode and opencode has `animations_enabled` with a `⋯` spinner fallback. The plan needs: screen mode (alt-screen vs `split-footer` + scrollback — framework-options §13 open question) treated as an a11y input, an animations-off/`NO_COLOR` mode, OSC 9/terminal-title notification policy, and which of these are registered settings keys (`window` vs `application`) versus env — all subject to the "no inert key" rule.
Where: `CLAUDE.md` Loading And Empty States; `packages/ui/src/components/{spinner,orbit-loader,ring-loader,loading-state}.tsx`; OpenTUI `renderer.d.ts:40-60` (`screenMode`), `scrollback.d.ts`; codex `tui/src/history_ui.rs`, `history_cell/mod.rs:171-290` (`raw_lines`), `insert_history.rs:1-5,105-215`; opencode `src/component/spinner.tsx:17-24`, `src/attention.ts`; `packages/contracts/src/settings/keys.ts` (registry rule).
